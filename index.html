The kit

A Pokédex that knows your cards.

Most trackers stop at "caught." Dexeon goes to the card level: every printing, its price, and the one you want to show off, all wired back to the species grid.

図鑑 · Pokédex

Catch 'em all, literally.

All 1025 species from Kanto to Paldea in one grid. Tap the circle to mark a Pokémon caught, star your favorites, and watch the completion bar climb. Filter by status or generation, search by name or number, switch between thumbnails and a compact list.

Every printing

Open a Pokémon, get every card.

Each species page lists every card ever printed for it, newest set first, pulled from the pokemontcg.io database. Search by set, rarity or card number, or flip on "Collected only" to see just what you own.

Know what it's worth.

Chase list

Hunt with a target.

Tap Chase on any card you're after. Your chase board totals the estimated value of the hunt, and chased Pokémon show up in grey on the home grid until you land them. Collect the card and the chase completes itself.

Showcase

Pick the face of each Pokémon.

Mark one collected card as the showcase and it becomes that species' tile on your home grid. Your Pokédex slowly turns into a wall of the exact cards you own.

Offline

Fast on the train.

Collected and showcased card art is stored on your device permanently. The rest is cached generously, so scrolling all 1025 tiles stays smooth and revisits are instant.

Appearance

Day and night.

Light and dark modes, thumbnails or list, one tap from the filter bar. Built natively for iPhone.

Binders

Binders that page like binders.

Build as many as you like. Each page is a real 2×2 or 3×3 sheet of pockets, so what you lay out on screen matches what sits on your shelf. Any card from any set can go in a pocket, whether you own it yet or not.

  1. Name it and pick a layout."Charizard Collection," 3×3. Or 2×2 for oversized full arts.
  2. Tap an empty pocket, search any card.Type a name, tap the printing you want. It's placed and saved instantly.
  3. Drag to rearrange, add pages as you go.Drop a card on another pocket to swap them. Tap a filled pocket to open it with live pricing.

Accounts · optional

Sign in only if you want to.

Dexeon is fully usable as a guest: your Pokédex, chase list and binders live on your device. Sign in with email or Google when you want your collection backed up and waiting on your next iPhone, and everything you've already collected comes with you.

  • Guest mode with no sign-up wall
  • Backed up and synced when you sign in
  • Guest data is claimed by your account on first sign-in
  • No ads, no tracking, no subscriptions
Solo dev

Built by one trainer.

Dexeon is a solo project by Bree Pear, a collector who wanted the binder on the shelf and the Pokédex on the phone to finally agree with each other. It's free, and it stays free. No pro tier, no unlock, no "premium binders."

Card data and pricing come from pokemontcg.io and TCGplayer. Official artwork comes from PokéAPI. The rest was designed on a golden-ratio grid, one Fibonacci spacing step at a time.
