The kit

A tracker that knows your cards.

Most trackers stop at "caught." Dexeon goes to the card level: every printing, its price, the copies you hold, and the one you want to show off, all wired back to the species grid.

図鑑 · Full Dex

Complete the Full Dex.

All 1025 species across nine generations in one grid. Tap the ring to mark one caught, star your favorites, and watch the bar climb. Filter by status or generation, search by name or number, switch between thumbnails and a compact list, light or dark.

Every printing

Open a species, get every card.

Each species page lists every card ever printed for it, English and Japanese, newest set first. Search within a species by set, rarity or number, browse whole sets, or dig through the entire catalog with Advanced Search.

Camera

Point. Shoot. Matched.

Snap a card or pick a photo and Dexeon identifies the exact printing, then opens it so you can collect, chase, trade or showcase it. The camera comes straight back up for the next one, so a whole stack goes in fast.

Chase list

Hunt with a target.

Tap Chase on any card you're after. Your chase board totals the estimated value of the hunt, sorts and filters it, and chased species show up in gray on the home grid until you land them. Collect the card and the chase completes itself.

Showcase

Pick the face of each species.

Mark one collected card as the showcase and it becomes that species' tile on your home grid. Your Full Dex slowly turns into a wall of the exact cards you own.

Collection value

Watch it grow.

Your profile totals the market value of every copy you own and charts it over time, refreshed once a day, so you can see what a good month of hunting did.

Direct messages

Message any trainer on your team. Cards and binders travel as attachments that open in the app on tap, price and all, so a trade conversation never has to leave the thread.

Trade board

Tap Trade on a card you own and it's on the board for everyone to browse. Cards on your chase list wear a Wanted badge, and one tap messages the owner with the card attached.

Gym Leaders

One board for every trainer on the app, ranked by Full Dex, sets completed, this week's catches, or the arcade high score. A podium for the top three and a pinned pill that shows your rank.

Trainer profiles

An @username, a bio, a favorite 'mon, and links to Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Whatnot or eBay. Share a trainer card, compare stats head to head, and keep binders private, friends-only or public.

Built for a friendly hobby. Community guidelines before your first message, a content check on every send, block and report from any profile or thread, and push notifications you can mute. Ordinary trash talk is fine. Hate and threats aren't.

Binders

Binders that page like binders.

Build as many as you like. Each page is a real 2×2, 3×3 or 4×3 sheet of pockets, so what you lay out on screen matches what sits on your shelf. Start blank or from a whole set, drop in any card whether you own it yet or not, and hand the finished thing to a friend or put it on paper.

  1. Name it and pick a layout."Charizard Collection," 3×3. Or start from a set and the binder sizes itself and fills with every card, in black and white until you catch them.
  2. Tap a pocket, fill it three ways.Search any card from any set, scan the physical card, or upload your own photo for something the catalog doesn't know.
  3. Drag to rearrange, add pages as you go.Drop a card on another pocket to swap them. Tap a filled pocket to open it with live pricing.
  4. Share it or print it.Set it to private, friends-only or public. Publish a link anyone can open in a browser, send it in a message, or export a printable PDF or a CSV.

Accounts · optional

Sign in only if you want to.

Dexeon is fully usable as a guest: your Full Dex, chase list and binders live on your device. Sign in with email or Google when you want your collection backed up, a public profile, and the social side of the app. Everything you collected as a guest comes with you.

  • Guest mode with no sign-up wall
  • Backed up and synced when you sign in
  • Guest data is claimed by your account on first sign-in
  • Push notifications for messages and requests, muteable in Settings
  • Delete your account and everything in it whenever you like
  • No ads, no tracking, no subscriptions. One-time $5.99 at launch, free during the beta
Solo dev

Built by one trainer.

Dexeon is a solo project by Bree Pear, a collector who wanted the binder on the shelf and the tracker on the phone to finally agree with each other. Beta testers use it free. At public launch it becomes a one-time $5.99 purchase on the App Store: no subscription, no pro tier, no "premium binders."

Card data, images and pricing come from Scrydex and TCGplayer, covering English and Japanese sets. The rest was designed on a golden-ratio grid, one Fibonacci spacing step at a time, in the same newsprint style you're looking at now.
