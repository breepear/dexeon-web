Privacy Policy

Effective September 10, 2026 · Applies to the Dexeon iOS app and dexeontcg.com

The short version

  • Dexeon has no ads. It's free during the beta, free for life for a select group of early adopters, and a one-time $5.99 purchase for everyone else at public launch. Never a subscription. We don't sell your data, we don't run advertising or analytics SDKs, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can use the app without an account. In guest mode your collection stays on your device. The only things that leave it are requests for card data and, if you use the scanner, the photo you take.
  • If you sign in, your collection syncs to our cloud database (Google Firebase) so it's backed up and available on your other devices.
  • If you create a public profile, your username, display name, avatar, bio, favorite species, social links, and collection stats become visible to other signed-in Dexeon users, and you can appear on leaderboards. You can turn discoverability off.
  • Messages, trade listings and shared binders are user content. Messages are private to the two people in the conversation. Trade listings are visible to signed-in users. Public binders are visible to anyone with the link, including on the web.
  • You can delete your account from inside the app. Settings → Delete Account.

1. Who we are and what this covers

Dexeon is an independent, fan-made trading card collection tracker built and operated by Bree Pear, a sole developer based in the United States ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Dexeon iOS app, the website at dexeontcg.com, shared binder pages at dexeontcg.com/b/…, and the beta sign-up form on the website (together, the "Service").

Dexeon is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Nintendo, Game Freak, Creatures Inc., or The Pokémon Company. Card data and images come from licensed third-party sources described below.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Push notification token. If you allow notifications, Apple issues a device token that we register with Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can notify you of messages and friend requests. Invalid tokens are pruned automatically.
  • Timestamps and identifiers. Records above carry created and updated times and a random Firebase user id. We use the user id, not your email, to link your data.
  • Service logs. Firebase and Google Cloud keep standard operational logs for the backend, and Vercel keeps standard web-server access logs for dexeontcg.com (IP address, user agent, requested page, time). We don't add tracking of our own on top of these.
  • On-device storage. The app stores your collection in a local database, caches card images and card data on your device so screens load quickly, and remembers settings such as light or dark mode. Guest-mode data lives only here.

2.3 What we do not collect

  • No precise or approximate location.
  • No contacts, calendar, health or motion data.
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA) and no cross-app tracking. The app does not ask for tracking permission because it doesn't track.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. We don't measure how you use the app.
  • No payment information. Dexeon is free during the beta. At public launch it is sold as a one-time purchase through the App Store; Apple processes the payment and we never receive your card or billing details. There are no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

3. How we use it

  • To run the app: show your collection, chase list and binders, sync them across your devices when signed in, and restore them when you sign in on a new device.
  • To provide social features you opt into: your profile, friend connections, messaging, the trade board, shared binders and the Gym Leaders leaderboard.
  • To keep the community safe: run an automated hate and violent-threat check on messages before they send and again on the server, enforce a per-sender rate limit, act on reports, and honor blocks.
  • To notify you: push notifications for new messages and friend requests, which you can mute in Settings.
  • To communicate about the beta: if you gave us your email on the website, to send TestFlight release notes and ask for feedback.
  • To protect the Service: prevent abuse, debug problems, and comply with law.

We don't use your data to build advertising profiles, and we don't train machine-learning models on your content.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and collection data: for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account deletes it (see section 7).
  • Guest-mode data: stays on your device until you delete it in the app or remove the app. If you later sign in, it's claimed by your account and synced.
  • Messages: kept for both participants for the life of the conversation. Hiding a conversation removes it from your inbox only.
  • Trade listings: until you remove the listing or delete your account.
  • Reports: kept for as long as needed to review and to keep a record of enforcement actions, then deleted.
  • Push tokens: until you sign out, disable notifications, or the token is invalidated by Apple.
  • Beta sign-up emails: until the app launches publicly, or earlier if you ask us to remove yours.
  • Scanned photos: not stored by us.
  • Backups and logs: provider backups and operational logs may hold copies for a limited period, typically up to 30 days, before being overwritten.

7. Your choices and rights

Controls inside the app

  • Use guest mode. Don't sign in and nothing about your collection leaves your device.
  • Share Publicly toggle. Turn it off in Edit Profile to leave search results and the global leaderboard.
  • Binder visibility. Every binder is private, friends-only or public. Public links can be turned off with "Stop Sharing".
  • Mute notifications. Settings → Mute Message Notifications, or turn off notifications for Dexeon in iOS Settings.
  • Block and report. From any profile, message or trade listing.
  • Export. Any binder can be exported as a PDF or a CSV from its menu.
  • Edit or remove profile details at any time in Edit Profile.
  • Delete your account. Settings → Delete Account. You'll confirm by typing DELETE. This removes your sign-in, your collection entries, chase list, binders, published binder links, value history, custom card photos and profile photo from our servers and from the device, and clears your public profile, username, stats, trade listings, friendships, blocks and push tokens. Messages you sent remain in the recipient's copy of the conversation, attributed to a deleted account.

Rights you may have

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Most of these you can exercise directly in the app. For anything else, email us (section 13) and we'll respond within 30 days. We will never discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Security

Data travels between the app, the website and our providers over TLS. Cloud data is protected by Firebase security rules that restrict every record to its owner, with narrowly defined exceptions for the social features described above (for example, a public profile is readable by signed-in users, a public binder by anyone with its id). Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us. Messages pass through an automated content check before and after sending. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your data we'll notify you as the law requires.

9. Children

Dexeon is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. Creating an account, a public profile, or sending messages requires that you are at least 13 and old enough to consent to data processing where you live (16 in much of the EU). If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International transfers

Dexeon is operated from the United States and our providers store data there. If you use Dexeon from outside the United States, your information is transferred to and processed in the United States, where privacy laws may differ from those in your country. Our providers rely on standard contractual safeguards for such transfers.
